MY STORY

A life of practice

More than sixty thousand hours of meditation. Almost two decades in Asia: twelve and a half years in India and eight in Thailand. Masters of Kriya Yoga, shaktipat, Vedic meditation and Buddhism, the Kalachakra of His Holiness the Dalai Lama, and an awakening to the Self that has not reversed in nearly thirty years.

And then, much later, the Descent of the Flame. From there were born the Flame Codex and the work I share today.
